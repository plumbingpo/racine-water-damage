Drying a home is only half the job. Once the moisture readings are back to normal, there are still cut walls, missing flooring and bare subfloor to deal with. Water damage repair is the rebuild. Our team in Racine replaces what could not be saved and matches it to what was there before, so the room looks like a room again and not a job site.

Repair work covers a wide range. A minor loss can need just a couple of feet of wallboard plus baseboard and a coat of paint. At the bigger end, expect doors, trim, cabinet boxes, hardwood or laminate flooring, subfloor, full drywall sheets and insulation. We replace materials like for like where possible, and we tell you plainly when an exact match is not available so you can choose.

Dry first, then cover: that order never changes. A moisture meter has to show normal levels in the subfloor and framing before new material is installed. Mold hidden in a newly painted wall usually traces back to someone covering damp lumber. You also receive a written scope and schedule ahead of the work, which spells out what gets rebuilt and when.

Our Water Damage Repair Process

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle water damage repair from start to finish:

1

Confirm It Is Dry

We take final moisture readings on framing, subfloor and remaining drywall. Until the readings are normal for every material, no reconstruction happens, and no moisture gets trapped.

2

Put the Scope in Writing

You receive a room by room list of what will be replaced, the materials we plan to use and the expected schedule. On jobs with an active claim, our team reconciles that scope with the adjuster.

3

Rebuild the Structure

Insulation goes back into open cavities, drywall is hung, taped and finished, and subfloor is repaired. After that we install trim, cabinetry and floors, ordering the work so completed surfaces are not damaged.

4

Finish and Walk Through

The last stage covers texture matching, primer, paint and putting hardware, doors and baseboards back. Together we then walk through each room, and every listed item is corrected before the job is closed.

Signs It Is Time for Water Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • At floor level, cabinet panels and bases show swelling, peeling or soft spots.
  • A door frame or slab swelled, so the door rubs or fails to close.
  • Hardwood boards have cupped, crowned or lifted and did not flatten after drying.
  • Wall cavities remain open where drywall was removed for drying.
  • Ceiling drywall is sagging, cracked or crumbling after a leak from above.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • Baseboards and door casings are warped or no longer sit flat against the wall.

How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.
  • Run the bath fan during showers and for a while afterward so rebuilt walls stay dry.
  • Give fresh paint, caulk and flooring adhesive time to cure before wet mopping or heavy use.
  • Ask for final moisture readings before any contractor closes up a wall or floor.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.

Why Water Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

Rebuilding after a water loss differs from a normal remodel. The person rebuilding needs to know what the water did, where it went and whether the structure behind the wall is truly dry. Our team works from the moisture records taken during drying, so we know which studs were wet, which subfloor was treated and where to look twice. Knowing the background stops issues from being hidden behind new wallboard and a coat of paint.

The second skill is matching. A patch that shows is a repair you notice every day. Wall texture is blended, paint is feathered out to natural breaks, and flooring is sourced to line up with the boards already there when available. If nothing matches, you get options from us, for example new flooring run to a doorway. Our team pulls any permits the job needs and sends updates to your insurance company.
