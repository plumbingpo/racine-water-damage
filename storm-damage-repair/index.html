How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain

Why Professional Storm Damage Repair Matters

A roof leak that looks minor from inside may have done extensive hidden damage to insulation, roof decking and wall cavities, and that leads to mold growth and structural deterioration if it is not found and dried. Our technicians trace where the water traveled using thermal imaging plus moisture meters, so no affected area is overlooked.

Tarping and boarding up only work when they are done with the right technique. Tarps put on the wrong way may funnel water into the building or tear loose in the wind. Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Racine

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
